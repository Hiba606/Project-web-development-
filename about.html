About Me

My Journey.

Academic Goals

I am passionate about pursuing a degree in marketing. My academic journey at Brigham Young University (BYU) is one of growth and ambition. I am committed to excelling in my studies and applying what I learn to make a meaningful impact in the field of marketing. My long-term goal is to work in brand strategy or digital marketing, helping companies connect authentically with their audiences and grow their presence in a competitive global market.

Tennis Journey

Playing tennis has taught me valuable lessons in teamwork, strategic thinking, and perseverance—skills that are essential in the marketing world. Competing at various levels has helped me build a competitive mindset, which I now apply in my marketing strategies to stay ahead of industry trends and deliver impactful campaigns.

Background and Passions

Growing up in Tunisia, I developed an early interest in the power of communication and how brands connect with people. I realized that marketing isn’t just about selling products; it's about creating meaningful experiences and building relationships with consumers. My passion for creativity, problem-solving, and understanding consumer psychology naturally led me to marketing. I love exploring how digital tools can influence consumer behavior, and I’m excited to learn more about data-driven marketing strategies.

From Tunisia to BYU

Moving from Tunisia to study in the United States was a pivotal moment in my life. The opportunity to attend Brigham Young University allowed me to immerse myself in a diverse community, enhancing my understanding of global markets. This experience has shaped my approach to marketing by encouraging me to think beyond local borders and understand the global dynamics that influence consumer behavior. At BYU, I’m gaining a well-rounded perspective on how cultural, technological, and economic factors impact marketing strategies.
